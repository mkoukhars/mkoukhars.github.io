Biohack 2025 Website - MAYLA

My team participated in the Synapsis Biohackathon where we were awarded third place. The problem we focused on was that many patients eager to participate in clinical trials struggle to find opportunities due to lack of awareness, slow recruitment, and accessibility barriers. Our solution was a physician–patient platform to bridge this gap. I developed the demo prototype and coded the platform enabling physicians to upload patient files and match patients with relevant clinical trials while providing a support community.
